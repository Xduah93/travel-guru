About Travel Guru

Travel Guru is a group-first travel planning platform designed to make organizing trips feel easier and more intentional. It helps travelers align on destinations, budgets, and experiences so there is less confusion, less back-and-forth, and a smoother planning process overall.

The site is built for people who love traveling with friends, whether that means a quick getaway, a culture-driven trip, or a more social destination experience. The goal is to make group travel feel more organized, more inspiring, and a lot less stressful.
